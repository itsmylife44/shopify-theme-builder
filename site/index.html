How it works

From one prompt to a delivered theme, in one agent session.

  1. Setup

    Checks Node, Git and the Shopify CLI, connects your store or creates a free development store in your country, and asks every store decision in one message: languages, currency, markets, menus, shipping and returns.

  2. Brief

    Reads your reference, asks only what's missing (your brand's world, your shopper, three emotions, what the brand rejects) and reads the brief back for you to confirm.

  3. Three Directions

    Creates the theme from Shopify's Skeleton with its own Git history, uploads your photos, and writes three complete designs, each with its own home page and your text.

  4. You choose

    Switch the live preview between the three in the Studio and press Choose. The agent carries on by itself the moment you do.

  5. Build and review

    Composes the product, collection, cart, search, blog and other pages, writes every text in your language, then screenshots, audits and fixes before giving a PASS or HOLD verdict.

  6. Delivery

    Runs Lighthouse and Theme Check, then uploads the theme unpublished, packages a zip, or connects Shopify's GitHub integration. Publishing stays your call.

Three Directions, not one guess

A single AI design is a coin toss. You get three, live on your own store.

Each Direction decides type, color strategy, shape, spacing, product cards, media, motion, the home page's composition and one signature section. The three must differ in kind on at least three of those, checked against a list of generic-design tells before you see them. The chosen one's rules go into DIRECTION.md, so whoever edits the theme later knows why it looks the way it does.

The Studio

Your real theme, rendered by Shopify, editable with a click.

  • Click to edit. Any section, header and footer included: color scheme, text, layout, collections, menus and links. Every edit applies live.
  • Add sections from the catalog, picking a layout from wireframe thumbnails, or the Custom Sections your agent wrote.
  • Directions, Brand and Style. Compare and choose; tune fonts, color schemes, type scale, shape, spacing, cards and motion.
  • Every page, on desktop and mobile: home, product, collection, cart, search, blog, article, contact, 404.
  • Safe to experiment. Undo and redo, Save as a Git checkpoint, a live Theme Check status, and Download zip.

FAQ

Questions

Do I need to know Liquid or how to code?

No. You talk to your agent and click in the Studio. The theme is still plain Liquid in a Git folder, so a developer can take over any time.

What does it cost?

The plugin is free and MIT licensed. You pay only for the agent you already use, and a Shopify development store is free.

Will it change my live store?

No. The preview runs on a development theme and delivery uploads the theme unpublished. Publishing is always your decision, in Shopify admin.

Can my shop be in another language, or several?

Yes. The agent writes the page text in your default language, adds the theme's translation files for every language you sell in and enables them on the store. Layouts mirror for right-to-left languages.

I'm building for a client. How does the hand-over work?

Build on a development store, then deliver to the client's store with a collaborator account, or send them the zip from the Studio's Download zip button.

What do I need installed?

Node.js 22.12 or newer, Git, the Shopify CLI 4.8 or newer and Google Chrome. The agent checks all of them first and tells you how to fix what's missing.
